Rome Battle AI - modes and commands

Polski · Commanders catalogue

A short cheat sheet: which modes this fork has and how to start them. Type every command in PowerShell, in the mod's folder:

cd C:\rome-battle-ai

The AI server must be running before the battle starts. Leave its window open while you play and stop it with Ctrl+C. Changed options take effect only after the server is started again.

1. Who commands which side

ModeCommandWhat happens
You against Claude.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.server --backend claude-code --effort mediumClaude commands the computer's side, you command yours.
Claude against Claude.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.server --backend claude-code --effort medium --sides allEvery side of the battle gets its own Claude. You only watch.
3-4 teamsthe same command with --sides allEvery team of the custom battle gets its own Claude. All fight all.

With --sides all, do not give orders to your own units - you would fight Claude for them. Move the camera as much as you like. Pausing the game pauses the generals.

A team is not an army: if a team has two armies, one Claude commands both.

2. The general's character (--personality)

Every general, the standard one too, knows which faction he leads, whom he fights and how such armies fought each other in history. --personality adds the style of a particular commander. Without it, the general follows the standard tactics of strategy.md.

What you wantAdd to the server command
The standard generalnothing
The standard general who never retreats and, once the battle cannot be won, kills as many enemies as he can--personality no_retreat
The Berserker: fights to the death from the start, only the kills count--personality berserker
One commander for every side--personality hannibal
Different commanders for different sides--personality 0=hannibal 1=scipio
Each general chooses a commander for his army and his enemy--personality auto
A mix--personality 0=caesar 1=auto

Side numbers: 0 is the first team, 1 the second, and so on. When the battle starts, the server prints took control of alliance N and a line [alliance N] army: ...; personality: ... - there you can see which side has which number and whom it got.

The commanders with their descriptions are in the catalogue. Just the names:

.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.recruit --list

3. Model and thinking time

OptionWhat it does
--model claude-sonnet-5-5The default model. Fast and good.
--model claude-opus-5-5Stronger, slower, uses up the usage limit faster.
--effort low / medium / highHow long the general thinks before each plan.

The battle does not wait for the general: a high effort means longer thinking while the units fight without new orders. Two sensible settings: Sonnet with medium, or Opus with low.

Usage: every side with Claude is a separate general, so --sides all with two teams uses the limit twice as fast, with four teams four times as fast. When the limit runs out, the game's own AI takes the army; the general tries to come back every minute.

4. Picking an army before the battle

Claude picks an army within the custom battle budget and writes a sheet you click it together from.

.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.recruit --faction macedon --enemy romans_julii --budget 15000 --battle field
OptionMeaning
--factionYour faction, e.g. macedon, carthage, romans_julii (full list: --list)
--enemyThe enemy faction, or several: --enemy romans_scipii gauls
--budgetDenarii, as set in the custom battle
--battlefield (field battle), siege-attack (you attack a town), siege-defense (you defend a town)
--personalityA commander, or auto (the default). The commander decides which units are picked.
--unitsMost units in the army (default 20, what an army holds)
--langLanguage of the sheet: pl (default) or en
--model, --effortModel for the picking (default Sonnet, medium)

The result goes to C:\rome-battle-ai\armies\, as .html and .md. The sheet has:

  • the units with their names as in the game's menu, counts and prices,
  • the chosen commander and why,
  • how to use the army,
  • a ready server command with the same commander.

Prices are the basic level, without weapon, armour or experience upgrades - check the total in the game's menu.

5. Ready setups

You against a Claude who never gives up:

.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.server --backend claude-code --effort medium --personality no_retreat

Hannibal against Scipio:

.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.server --backend claude-code --effort medium --sides all --personality 0=hannibal 1=scipio

Four teams, each general chooses his commander:

.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.server --backend claude-code --effort medium --sides all --personality auto

The full cycle: Claude picks both armies and fights with them:

  1. Pick an army for each side:
.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.recruit --faction carthage --enemy romans_scipii --budget 12000 --battle field --lang en
.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.recruit --faction romans_scipii --enemy carthage --budget 12000 --battle field --lang en
  1. Click both armies together in the custom battle from the sheets in armies\.
  1. Start the server with the commanders from the sheets, e.g.:
.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.server --backend claude-code --effort medium --sides all --personality 0=hannibal 1=scipio
  1. Start the battle and watch.

6. Where things are

File or folderWhat is in it
strategy.mdThe standard tactics every general follows. You can edit them.
rules.mdHard facts about the game.
personalities.mdArmy types, their factions and the commanders. Add your own commander here.
battles.mdThe battles of antiquity.
docs\commanders.htmlThe catalogue of commanders, army types and battles, to read.
armies\The army sheets from the picker.
logs\A record of every battle: what the general saw and what he decided.

The server reads every .md file before each battle, so no restart is needed. After editing personalities.md or battles.md, check them and rebuild the catalogue:

.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.personalities check
.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.personalities docs

7. When something does not work

SymptomWhat to do
No hello: rome-remastered v1 line in the server windowCheck that dinput8.dll is in the game's folder, next to Total War ROME REMASTERED.exe.
general failed: You've hit your session limitThe Claude usage limit ran out. The game's AI commands the army; the general comes back by itself when the limit resets.
Units stand without ordersLook at the server window's log.
You want the original mod backCopy C:\rome-battle-ai\hook-windows\dinput8.dll (v0.3) into the game's folder and start the server without --sides and --personality.

Tests without the game (they use no Claude limit):

.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.personalities check
.venv\Scripts\python -m rome_ai.recruit --check
